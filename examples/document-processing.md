# AI-Assisted Document Processing

## Overview

This example demonstrates how Artificial Intelligence can assist with processing large amounts of textual information.

The workflow is designed for situations where users need to extract, organize, compare, summarize, and analyze information from documents.

The objective is to reduce repetitive information-processing work while maintaining human verification.

---

## 1. Problem

Professionals often need to work with multiple documents containing information in different formats and structures.

Typical tasks include:

- Reading lengthy documents
- Finding specific information
- Comparing requirements
- Extracting dates and numbers
- Identifying responsibilities
- Summarizing content
- Preparing structured notes

These tasks can consume significant amounts of time.

AI can assist with the initial processing and organization of information.

---

## 2. Workflow

```text
Source documents
        ↓
