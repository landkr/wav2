# AI Training and Knowledge Sharing

## Overview

This example describes how Artificial Intelligence can be used to support training, education, and knowledge sharing.

The project focuses on practical AI literacy rather than simply introducing AI concepts.

The objective is to help users understand how to identify suitable AI tasks, design effective workflows, evaluate AI-generated information, and use AI responsibly.

---

## 1. Training Challenge

Many people have access to AI tools but do not know how to apply them effectively to their daily work.

Common challenges include:

- Unclear understanding of what AI can and cannot do
- Poorly defined tasks
- Ineffective prompts
- Lack of output verification
- Difficulty integrating AI into existing workflows
- Concerns about privacy and information security

Practical training can help address these challenges.

---

## 2. Training Approach

The project uses a task-oriented learning model.

```text
Real-world task
       ↓
Identify where AI can help
       ↓
Design an AI-assisted workflow
       ↓
Test the workflow
       ↓
Evaluate the output
       ↓
Improve the workflow
       ↓
Apply it to real work
