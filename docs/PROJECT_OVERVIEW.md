# Project Overview

## 1. Background

Artificial Intelligence is rapidly becoming an important component of digital transformation.

However, access to AI tools alone does not guarantee successful adoption. Organizations and individuals need practical workflows, appropriate training, reliable information, and clear guidance on how AI can be integrated into real-world work.

This project focuses on bridging that gap by developing and documenting practical applications of Artificial Intelligence for digital transformation.

## 2. The Problem

Many organizations are currently facing several challenges when adopting AI:

- Rapid changes in AI technology make it difficult to keep knowledge up to date.
- Staff may have access to AI tools but lack practical experience.
- AI demonstrations do not always translate into repeatable workflows.
- Organizations need guidance on responsible and secure AI use.
- Successful experiments are often not documented in a reusable form.

As a result, there is a gap between **having access to AI technology** and **being able to use AI effectively in everyday work**.

## 3. Project Approach

The project follows a practical, experiment-driven approach.
