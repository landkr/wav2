## 1. AI-Assisted Document Processing

### Problem

Professionals often need to review, summarize, compare, and extract information from large numbers of documents.

These tasks can require significant amounts of time, particularly when information is distributed across multiple documents or presented in different formats.

### AI-Assisted Workflow

```text
Source documents
       ↓
AI extracts relevant information
       ↓
Information is structured
       ↓
AI identifies key points
       ↓
Human verifies the results
       ↓
Final output
