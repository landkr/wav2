# How Claude Will Be Used

## Overview

Claude will be used as an advanced AI assistant within the AI for Digital Transformation project.

The project focuses on applying Artificial Intelligence to practical work, digital transformation, training, knowledge management, and information processing.

Claude will support experimentation, workflow development, research, documentation, and training-resource creation.

---

## 1. AI-Assisted Research

Claude will be used to assist with:

- Organizing research questions
- Analyzing complex information
- Comparing approaches
- Summarizing research materials
- Identifying areas requiring further investigation
- Structuring research findings

Human review will be used to verify important information.

---

## 2. Document Analysis

Claude will be used to experiment with advanced document-processing workflows.

Potential tasks include:

- Summarizing long documents
- Extracting structured information
- Comparing documents
- Identifying requirements
- Organizing information
- Converting unstructured information into structured outputs

The project will evaluate the accuracy and usefulness of these workflows.

---

## 3. AI Workflow Development

Claude will be used to design and test repeatable AI-assisted workflows.

The development process will generally follow:

```text
Real-world problem
       ↓
Workflow design
       ↓
Claude-assisted processing
